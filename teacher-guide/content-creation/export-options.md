# Export Options

**Exporting Your Exercise**

You can download your exercise in three formats — perfect for printing, reference, or extra practice.<br>

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-03-24 at 7.52.24 AM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

| Format                | What You Get                                         | Use It For                                       |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| 📄 **Question Paper** | All questions formatted neatly for printing          | Offline exams, physical backups, parent meetings |
| 📝 **Answer Key**     | Complete answers with solutions                      | Quick grading reference, class discussions       |
| 📋 **Practice Sheet** | Questions only (no answers)  student-friendly layout | Homework, extra practice, revision               |

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-03-24 at 7.53.27 AM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

\
**How to export:**

1. Click the **Export** dropdown on the preview page
2. Choose your format
3. Wait a moment while the PDF is generated
4. The file opens in a new tab  save or print from there<br>

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-03-24 at 7.54.13 AM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

> ⚠️ If nothing happens when you click export, check if your browser is blocking pop-ups and allow them for this site.

### Tips for Creating Great Exercises

#### 💡 Best Practices

* Start with **AI topic suggestions** — they're based on your curriculum and save time
* Set a **2-day window** (the default) — it gives students enough time without dragging on
* Always **preview before approving** — once approved, you can't change the questions
* Think about your students' level when choosing **difficulty**
* Use **Upload Paper** mode for question papers you've already prepared on paper

#### 🎯 Choosing the Right Topics

* Mix AI-suggested topics with ones you've **recently taught in class**
* Remove any topics that are **too advanced** or not yet covered
* For revision exercises, use **Chapter Wise** mode to cover broader material

#### ✅ Before You Hit Approve

1. Read through **every question**  don't skip!
2. Check that **formulas and diagrams** look correct
3. Verify the **correct answers** are actually correct
4. Make sure the difficulty **matches your class level**
5. **Export a copy** for your records before approving
