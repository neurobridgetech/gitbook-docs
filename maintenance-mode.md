# Maintenance Mode

### What is Maintenance?

The Chanakya AI platform occasionally goes offline for updates and improvements. During maintenance, you can't access the platform, but your data stays safe and the platform returns better than before!\
\

### What You'll See

#### 🔔 **Before Maintenance**

* Warning banner with start/end times (in UTC)
* "Learn More" link for details
* Platform works normally

#### ⚙️ **During Maintenance**

* Chanakya AI logo and moving animation
* "Maintenance Break!" message with timing
* No platform access

#### ✅ **After Maintenance**

* Automatic return to your dashboard
* All features restored
*   Your data unchanged\

### Common Questions

**❓ "I was taking a test during maintenance"**\
Contact your teacher immediately. They can restore your session or provide alternatives.

**❓ "My assignment is due during maintenance"**\
Don't worry! Deadlines are automatically extended and teachers are notified.

**❓ "How do I know when it's back online?"**\
Check the maintenance page for end times, or just visit the website normally.

**❓ "Can I access downloaded materials?"**\
Yes! Any files saved to your device work offline during maintenance.

**❓ "What if maintenance takes longer?"**\
The page will update with new timing, and all deadlines will be adjusted accordingly.

### Understanding UTC Time

**All maintenance times use UTC (Universal Time).** To convert to your local time:

1. **Google:** "UTC to \[your city] time"
2. **Calculate:** Learn your UTC offset (e.g., India = UTC+5:30)
3. **Ask:** Your teacher can help with timing

**Examples:**

* UTC 2:00 AM = 7:30 AM in India
* UTC 2:00 AM = 9:00 PM (previous day) in New York

### Quick Troubleshooting

#### 🔧 **Can't Access Platform After Maintenance?**

1. Check internet connection
2. Refresh the page (F5)
3. Try different browser/device
4. Wait 15-30 minutes for full restart
5. Contact teacher if still not working

#### 😰 **Lost Work During Maintenance?**

1. Contact teacher immediately with details
2. Check for auto-saved drafts
3. Take screenshots of any errors
4. Don't panic - teachers will help
